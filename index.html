Web Development

Conceived for Claro — América Móvil (AMX/NYSE), the largest telecommunications company in Latin America — and Ogilvy Brazil. The solution was a social marketing campaign builder optimized for mobile devices, with extensive SEO and analytics support. It enabled Claro to become the first wireless carrier in Brazil to engage customers in social networks around the 2014 World Cup, while minimizing developer dependency for greater marketing agility.

PECE

Full Stack

The Platform for Experimental, Collaborative Ethnography (PECE) is an open source Drupal-based digital platform that supports multi-sited, cross-scale ethnographic and historical research. It links researchers in new ways, enables new kinds of analyses and data visualization, and activates researchers' engagement with public problems and diverse audiences.

PECE provides a place to archive and share primary data generated by scholars in the empirical humanities and social sciences, facilitates analytic collaboration, and encourages experimentation with diverse modes of publication and peer review.

  • Client: Taller Digital Business
  • Customer: UCI — University of California, Irvine
  • Role: Tech Lead, Drupal Architect
